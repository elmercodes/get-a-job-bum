# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (release candidate)

- Chrome side panel with paired loopback backend and explicit employer-page access.
- Reviewed applicant evidence, resume import, accomplishment records, and scoped reusable answers.
- Sourced public company research with posting requirements and inference shown separately.
- Reviewed and validated Word tailoring tied to approved evidence, with optional PDF rendering on supported Linux setups.
- Multi-step form help, per-application attachment and consent review, separate submit approval, and receipt-based tracking.
- Local application history, CSV, and opt-in Google Sheets sync with offline recovery and conflict handling.
- Reproducible fictional tests, local privacy checks, and MIT attribution to Auto Job and Application Assistant.

See the [v0.1.0 release notes](docs/RELEASE_NOTES_0.1.0.md) for setup and limitations.
